# Zepto-Dashboard

## Overview
This project shows the analysis of Zepto sales and other infromation leveraging the capabilities of Power BI. The dashboard helps in assessing the performance of the business with simple charts.

## Data & Tools
Data taken from Kaggle. Data is mostly clean, so no transformations used. Power BI Desktop used for Visualization.

## Dashboard Insights
**Metrics** - Net Sales Amount, Total Orders, Total Customers, Total Stores, Average Delivery in minutes, Average Amount per Order, Average Discount Amount per Order

**Charts** - Net Amount by Year and Month, Net Amount by Promotion Type, Net Amount by Category, Net Amount by Payment Method, Customers Sign up by Year and Stores Opening by Opening Year.

**Filter** - Date Raneg Filter, City Selection, Order Status Filter

## Analysis (As per the data)
Total net sale amount is 1.3Bn rupees with total customers of 25K and 180 stores established.  


Net amount by timeline chart shows that there is increase in sales in alternative months.
Net Amount by category explains that Beverages, Fruits and Personal care products most bought by customers.  


In promotions Flat and Free delivery leads the most transactions and UPI is the most favored payment method by Customers.
Customers sign up seems similar in numbers for 2024 and 2025 and Stores opening is most in 2021 and 2024.
